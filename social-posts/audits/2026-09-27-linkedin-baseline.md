# LinkedIn Account Baseline — 2026-09-27

This audit is immutable. Create a new dated audit after another 12–16 original posts rather than rewriting this snapshot.

## Scope and limitations

- **Source:** direct LinkedIn account retrieval
- **Retrieved:** 45 activities: 41 original posts and 4 reposts
- **Caveat:** this is everything exposed by the bounded deep retrieval, not a guaranteed lifetime archive
- **Unavailable:** per-post follows, profile views, saves, geography, and posting times

The evidence supports an audience-interest and conversation diagnosis, not exact follower attribution.

The text average is distorted by one 957-impression outlier. One video is insufficient to establish a format advantage.

## Strongest signals

- Timely named-product analysis: Claude Opus reached 957 impressions.
- Concrete developer decisions: library versus framework reached 637.
- Existing full-stack authority: Flutter/React hybrid architecture reached 543; Flutter internals reached 529.
- First-hand engineering pain: coding agents repeatedly reading a repository reached 502.
- Broad, practical mental models outperformed narrow acronym-heavy explanations.

The repeatable pattern was **specific problem + decision or failure + practical consequence**, not one universal format.

## Growth gaps

1. The follow promise was unclear across AI agents, MCP, RAG, Linux, sockets, Flutter, React, certificates, habits, and art.
2. Reach rarely became conversation; even the 957-impression post received no comments.
3. Too few posts showed working demos, traces, evaluations, cost, latency, or measured failures.
4. Repeated “Most developers...” hooks and generic questions made posts feel interchangeable.
5. Unsupported precision in CPU, latency, memory, or speed claims risked expert trust.
6. Certificate posts showed activity without demonstrating changed engineering judgment.

## Decisions produced by this audit

- Own the category **reliable AI product engineering**.
- Use single images as the default proof/mental-model format; reserve carousels for sequences and frameworks.
- Test at least three native videos before judging their value.
- Tie software and math foundations to explicit AI-product consequences.
- Optimize for qualified comments, profile views, and follower conversion—not impressions alone.
- Require a source, trace, experiment, or clear qualification for precise technical claims.
