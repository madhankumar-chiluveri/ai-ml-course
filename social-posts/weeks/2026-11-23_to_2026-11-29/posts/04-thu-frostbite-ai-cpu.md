## LinkedIn

### Final copy

AI agents designed a CPU. How did the team check their work?

Architect Labs says its AI systems worked across Frostbite's compiler, chip design and verification.

The part I found useful: the team describes a machine-checkable specification for the chip. As the design evolved, changes were checked against that shared specification.

A specification makes expected behavior explicit. Checks can then test whether parts of the design meet it. That does not prove the entire processor is free of errors.

The company also reports performance-per-watt estimates for selected workloads. Those are pre-silicon estimates, not measurements from a manufactured Frostbite processor.
