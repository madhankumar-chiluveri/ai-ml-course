## Source notes

- [Anthropic guide, 22 October 2026](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-agents-when-to-fold-subagents-into-your-main-agent-loop) describes conditions for combining specialist subagents and reasons to retain separate agents. [Official migration kit](https://github.com/anthropics/single-agent-loop-kit) provides a guided assessment workflow.
- This is source analysis, not an independent benchmark or production incident. Diagrams are conceptual illustrations; they show no measured performance results.
- Separate agents can remain useful for parallel work, fresh context, and verification. Fewer agents do not guarantee correct output. Recheck material source changes before publication.
